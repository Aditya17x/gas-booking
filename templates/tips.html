🔥 LPG SAFETY TIPS

📦 Cylinder Storage

  • Keep cylinder upright always
  • Store away from direct sunlight
  • Keep in well-ventilated area
  • Never store in bathroom
  • Keep away from heat sources

🔥 Fire Prevention

  • Never leave stove unattended
  • Keep curtains away
  • Have fire extinguisher
  • Clean grease regularly
  • Do not overload plug points

🌬 Proper Ventilation

  • Open windows while cooking
  • Use exhaust fan
  • Never block air vents
  • Allow fresh air flow
  • Keep kitchen airy

🔧 Regulator Safety

  • Check cracks regularly
  • Replace damaged pipe
  • Use ISI regulators
  • Turn off after use
  • Tighten properly

🧯 Emergency Handling

  • Do not panic
  • Turn off main valve
  • Open doors & windows
  • Do NOT use switches
  • Move outside if needed

👃 Gas Leak Detection

  • Smell for LPG odor
  • Apply soap solution
  • Bubbles mean leakage
  • Never use matchstick
  • Call gas agency

⚡ Electrical Safety

  • Keep wires away from stove
  • Do not touch with wet hands
  • Switch off after use
  • Use quality sockets

👶 Child Safety

  • Keep stove out of reach
  • Use safety locks
  • Do not allow kids near flame
  • Educate about danger
